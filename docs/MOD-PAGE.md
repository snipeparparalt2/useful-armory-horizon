# Useful Armory

A revival of my old Minecraft 1.12.2 mod for Horizon, with eight regular equipment families, Ultimate armor, tools, daggers, greatswords, halberds, and special weapons.

- 116 standalone items, 6 blocks, and 124 recipes.
- Three custom ores configured for new Overworld terrain, with matching cobblestone backgrounds.
- Emissive accents on worn Aquite/Shadow armor and their placed ores.
- Animated Ultimate inventory icons; static multicolor worn Ultimate armor.
- One `.hmod` download, installed from the mod browser like any content mod.
- Optional: [Armory Lib](https://github.com/snipeparparalt2/armory_lib), a separate native library, adds what needs code: the coloured gear light,
  set bonuses, sneak abilities, weapon effects and the new creatures' looks. Without it the items,
  ores, creatures and places all still work.

**Target:** Horizon nightly `fdc87ae`, Minecraft data 26.3.

Put the `.hmod` in `horizon-mods/`, enable Useful Armory, and restart. Do not put it in `horizon-native-mods/`. Armory Lib's `.hnmod` goes in `horizon-native-mods/` and needs a Build in the launcher.

Screenshots use Wisdom Horizon 1.3.7 shaders, which are optional and not included. Emissive textures stay bright themselves; they do not illuminate the surroundings. The current build does not support emissive overlays on held or inventory items.
